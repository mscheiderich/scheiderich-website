For Business Owners

Commercial Insurance

Commercial coverage tends to get bought in a hurry — a lease requires it, a client asks for a certificate, a job will not start without proof. Iris slows that down enough to explain what each piece is actually for, so you are not carrying coverage you do not need or missing something a contract assumes you have.

General liability responds when someone outside your business claims your work or your premises caused injury or damage. Commercial property covers the building, equipment, inventory, and fixtures you depend on. Commercial auto matters the moment a vehicle is used for work, because a personal auto policy may not respond to a business use claim — that surprise is one of the more common gaps she finds.

What the right combination looks like depends on what you do, where you do it, who comes onto your property, and what your contracts require. Iris works through those specifics rather than starting from a package, and she can review a policy you already carry to see whether it still matches how the business operates today. Common Questions

Questions Clients Ask Iris

¿Puedo hacer todo el proceso en español?
Sí. Iris is fully bilingual, so the quote, the paperwork, the questions afterward, and the renewal conversation can all happen in Spanish with the same person. You will not be passed to a translation line or asked to bring someone to interpret. If you would rather switch between English and Spanish, that is fine too.
Does my personal auto policy cover me when I drive for work?
Often it does not, and this is one of the more common gaps Iris finds. Personal auto policies frequently exclude or limit business use, which means a claim during a work trip may not respond the way an owner assumed. Whether you need commercial auto depends on how the vehicle is used, so it is worth describing your actual routine rather than guessing.
I am buying my first house in Georgia. What do I need before closing?
Your lender will want proof of a homeowners policy with specific details — the correct mortgagee wording, your loan number, and a dwelling limit that satisfies their requirements. Iris handles that paperwork directly with the lender. Starting the conversation when the contract is signed, rather than the week of closing, leaves room to fix anything that comes back wrong.
Can she handle both my business and my family's policies?
Yes, and coordinating them is often the point. When personal and commercial coverage sit with two different agents, gaps tend to appear at the seam — a vehicle used both ways, a home office, equipment stored at the house. Having one agent look at both makes those overlaps visible.
