About Mike

The Agent Behind the Agency

Mike founded The Scheiderich Agency with a focus on clarity, responsiveness, and professionalism. With years of experience working alongside Georgia homeowners, families, and business owners, he understands how insurance decisions affect people's lives and finances — not in the abstract, but in the specific months when a claim, a closing, or a loss actually lands.

Questions About Working With Mike

What areas does Mike serve?
Mike works with clients throughout Buford, Gwinnett County, and Metro Atlanta, and is licensed statewide in Georgia. The office is at 60 W Park St in Downtown Buford, and most conversations happen by phone or email rather than in person.
Do I need an appointment to talk with Mike?
No. Call 770-268-4418 during business hours or send the form on this page and someone will follow up, typically the same business day. If Mike is with another client, a member of the team can start the conversation and hand it back to him.
How do I know how much life insurance I need?
It depends on three things: your income, what you owe, and who depends on you. A common starting point is the DIME method, which adds up debt, income replacement, mortgage balance, and education costs for your children. Our calculator walks through those numbers in about two minutes and gives you an estimate to talk through. It is a starting point for a conversation, not a quote or an offer of coverage.
Can Mike review a policy I already have somewhere else?
Yes. Bring your current declarations page and he will walk through what it actually covers, where the gaps are, and whether the limits still match your situation. There is no charge for the review and no obligation to move anything.
Does Mike handle both personal and business coverage?
Yes. Many of his clients are business owners who need commercial coverage and personal home, auto, and life coverage handled together rather than by two separate agents who never speak to each other.
What happens after I submit the form?
You get a call or email back, usually the same business day. The first conversation is about understanding your situation. Nothing is bound and no coverage is in force until a policy is issued and you have confirmed it in writing.
