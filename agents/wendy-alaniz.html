About Wendy

The Part That Comes After the Policy

Most insurance attention goes to the sale. Wendy's work sits mainly on the other side of it — the call when a vehicle is added mid-term, the question about why a bill changed, the change of address that has to be handled before it causes a problem, the confusion about what a renewal notice is actually saying.

She is licensed to quote and bind, so she is not a message-taker who passes you along. If you call about a change, she can usually handle it in that conversation rather than scheduling a callback with somebody else. Clients who dislike explaining their situation twice tend to notice the difference.

Fully bilingual, Wendy handles that ongoing support in English or Spanish. For families who bought a policy in Spanish, being able to ask a follow-up question two years later in the same language — without arranging an interpreter or waiting for a call back — is usually the reason they stay.

Common Questions

Questions Clients Ask Wendy

I need to add a car mid-term. How fast can that happen?
Often the same day, if you have the vehicle identification number, the year, make, and model, and know who will be driving it. Wendy is licensed to bind, so she can usually process the change during the call rather than logging a request for someone else. The exact effective date depends on the carrier's confirmation.
¿Puede ayudarme en español con mi póliza actual?
Sí, por supuesto. Wendy is fully bilingual, and that applies to ongoing service, not just the original sale. Billing questions, coverage changes, renewal notices, and anything you did not understand in a letter can all be handled in Spanish, with the same person each time.
My bill changed and I do not understand why. Who do I ask?
Her. Premium changes usually trace back to something specific — a coverage change, a driver added or removed, a renewal adjustment, or a change in how the policy is being paid. Wendy can pull up the file and walk through what moved. If the answer sits with the carrier, she will tell you that and help you get it.
Do I have to come into the office to make a change?
No. Most changes are handled by phone or email, and many clients never visit the office at all. If you would rather sit down in person at 60 W Park St in Downtown Buford, that is welcome — but it is not a requirement for anything routine.
