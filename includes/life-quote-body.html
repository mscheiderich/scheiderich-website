The options

Term, whole, or universal — what's the difference?

Term life

Coverage for a set period, such as 10, 20, or 30 years. Good fit when the need has an end date, like a mortgage or raising kids.

Whole life

Coverage for your lifetime with level premiums. Builds cash value over time. Costs more than term for the same coverage amount.

Universal life

Lifetime coverage with more flexibility in premiums and death benefit. Builds cash value, and how that value grows depends on the policy type and interest rates.

There's no single right answer. Many people combine types. will walk through the tradeoffs with you before you decide anything.

Product availability, features, and costs vary by carrier and by individual. Coverage is subject to underwriting.

Questions

Common questions

Is term or whole life better?

Neither is better across the board. Term usually costs less upfront for a set period; whole and universal last for life and build cash value. The right choice depends on what you're protecting and for how long.

Do I need a medical exam?

Some policies require one and some don't. It depends on the coverage amount, your age, and the product. will tell you what to expect for the options you're considering.

How long does it take to get covered?

Some policies can be approved quickly; others take several weeks for underwriting. will give you a realistic timeline for your situation.
