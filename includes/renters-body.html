Questions renters ask

What's an "interested party"?

It means your property gets notified by the insurance company if your policy cancels. It doesn't give them any coverage or access to your policy details.

How fast can I get proof for move-in?

Often the same day. Once you choose and buy a policy, proof can usually go out right away.

I already have a policy. Is that okay?

Probably. Make sure it meets your lease's liability limit and lists your property the way the lease asks. We're happy to look it over with you.

I have a roommate. Do we each need a policy?

Usually, yes. Each person's belongings are typically covered on their own policy. will walk you through it.

Do I have to buy from you?

No. You can buy renters insurance from any provider. We're a fast, local option, and we handle the lease paperwork for you.
