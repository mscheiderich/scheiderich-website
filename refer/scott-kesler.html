When to send

Send at contract — not at clear-to-close

Corrections are what cost time, and corrections come from incomplete information handled in a hurry. Sending at contract means the loan number, the mortgagee clause and the dwelling figure are confirmed once, at a point where getting them right is routine rather than urgent.

Send it when:
  • The contract is signed, even if nothing else is settled
  • Your client says "I'll just use my current agent" — send it anyway, we'll coordinate
  • The loan amount changed
  • The home is 25+ years old or has an older roof
  • It's a rental, second home, or new construction
