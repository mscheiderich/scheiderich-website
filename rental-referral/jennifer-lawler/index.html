The problem

We know where move-ins get stuck

Renters insurance is a small part of a lease, and it still holds up move-ins, renewals and your team's time.

Proof isn't in on move-in day.

The keys are ready, the resident is waiting, and your team is on the phone chasing a document.

The property is listed wrong.

Wrong legal name, wrong notice address, or no listing at all, so the proof comes back to your desk to fix.

Coverage quietly lapses.

A resident cancels in month two and nobody knows until renewal, or until something happens.

Your staff become the help desk.

Leasing teams end up answering liability-limit questions they were never trained to answer.

Your requirements

Your property, listed the way your lease requires

When a resident's lease requires it, we list your property as an interested party on their policy, using the exact legal name and notice address you give us. That way you're notified if the policy cancels, instead of finding out at renewal.

Send us your requirements once:
  • The minimum liability limit your lease requires
  • Your property's legal name
  • The mailing address for interested-party notices
  • Where proof of insurance should be sent

We keep them on file and use them for each resident you send, so you don't have to repeat yourself.

Questions

Questions partners ask

Does it cost our property anything?

No. There's no cost to your property to refer residents.

What do you need from us?

Your lease's insurance requirement: the liability limit, your property's legal name, and the address for interested-party notices. Send it once and we'll keep it on file.

Will we know if a resident cancels?

As the interested party on the policy, your property is notified by the insurance company if the policy cancels.

How fast can a resident be covered?

If we reach them by 3 PM on a business day and they choose a policy, proof of insurance can usually go out the same day.

What if a resident already has a policy that doesn't list the property correctly?

We'll tell them exactly what needs to change. If the policy is with another company, we'll tell them what to ask their current agent.

Can you help Spanish-speaking residents?

Yes. Bilingual members of our team can help.

Do residents have to use you?

No. Residents are always free to buy coverage from any provider. Referring them to us just gives them a fast, local option.
