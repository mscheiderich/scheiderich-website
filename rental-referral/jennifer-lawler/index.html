Questions partners ask

Does it cost our property anything?

No. There's no cost to your property to refer residents.

What do you need from us?

Your lease's insurance requirement: the liability limit, your property's legal name, and the address for interested-party notices. Send it once and we'll keep it on file.

Will we know if a resident cancels?

As the interested party on the policy, your property is notified by the insurance company if the policy cancels.

How fast can a resident be covered?

If we reach them by 3 PM on a business day and they choose a policy, proof of insurance can usually go out the same day.

What if a resident already has a policy that doesn't list the property correctly?

We'll tell them exactly what needs to change. If the policy is with another company, we'll tell them what to ask their current agent.

What if our property is listed wrong on a resident's policy?

Tell us and we'll correct it the same business day.

What if Jennifer is out of the office?

Wendy or Crissy on our team picks up the referral, so your resident still hears from us on time.

Can you help Spanish-speaking residents?

Yes. Bilingual members of our team can help.

Do residents have to use you?

No. Residents are always free to buy coverage from any provider. Referring them to us just gives them a fast, local option.
